Admissions

How to apply to Star High School

We accept applications for Form I and Form V, and for other forms when places are available. Please call the school office to confirm current dates, requirements and fees.

  1. Send an inquiry

    Fill in the form below, or call either number to tell us which form you are applying for.

  2. The school office replies

    We read your inquiry and contact you by phone or email to explain the next steps.

  3. Submit documents

    Bring the birth certificate, recent passport photographs and previous school reports or results.

  4. Selection

    The school reviews each application. Applicants may be invited for an interview or placement assessment.

  5. Confirm and report

    Successful families receive a joining letter with dates, requirements and uniform. Students then report as boarders.
